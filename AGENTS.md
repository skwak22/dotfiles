# IMPORTANT
Never commit or push proprietary, internal, or company-specific details to public GitHub.
Dotfiles often collect machine-specific or internal configuration over time. Avoid pushing configs with:

* Internal hostnames, private IPs, or VPN addresses (e.g., *.corp.company.com)
* Internal source control paths, workspaces, or proprietary depot references
* Configurations, aliases, or CLI wrappers for internal infrastructure and proprietary tools
* Corporate email addresses, employee IDs, LDAP identifiers, or credentials
